## 3. Warranty

- **Mower unit:** 36 months from the date of purchase.
- **Lumen PowerCell L2 battery:** 5 years (60 months).
- **HomeBase Z charging dock:** 24 months.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects only. Damage caused by flooding, deliberate misuse, or
running the mower on slopes above 35% is **not** covered.

## 4. Returns and refunds

- Returns are accepted within **45 days** of delivery.
- Returns within the first **30 days** receive a **full refund**.
- Returns between day 31 and day 45 are subject to a **15% restocking fee**.
- The mower must be returned with the HomeBase Z dock and all boundary-wire accessories.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within **10 business days** of inspection.

## 6. Error codes

| Code | Meaning | Recommended action |
|---|---|---|
| **E-12** | Blade obstruction | Power off, remove debris from the blade disc, restart. |
| **E-27** | Boundary wire disconnected | Check the boundary-wire connectors at the HomeBase Z dock. |
| **E-40** | Low battery | The mower returns to the dock automatically to recharge. |
| **E-53** | Lifted / tilted | Place the mower back on level ground and press Start. |
| **E-61** | Dock not found | Verify the dock has power and the guide wire is intact. |

## 7. Firmware

- Firmware updates are released **quarterly** (March, June, September, December).
- Updates are downloaded automatically overnight while the mower is docked and connected to Wi-Fi.
- The current stable firmware line is **ZephyrOS 3.x**.

## 8. Frequently asked questions

**How do I set the mowing schedule?**
Open the Lumen Home app, select your Zephyr One, and use *Schedule → Add slot*. Up to 7 slots per
week are supported.

**Can the Zephyr One work without the boundary wire?**
No. The Zephyr One requires the installed boundary wire to define the mowing area. GPS-only
operation is not supported on this model.

**What should I do before winter?**
Fully charge the battery, clean the blade disc, and store the mower indoors above 5 °C. Leave the
HomeBase Z dock installed but disconnect its power supply.
